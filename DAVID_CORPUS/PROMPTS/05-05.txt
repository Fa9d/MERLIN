3. The Costly Signal Designer

Prompt:

Act as a signaling theorist applying Sutherland's principle that the most trusted
brand signals are the ones that would be expensive to fake. Words are cheap.
Costly signals are what audiences actually believe. The job is to find one thing |
can do that quietly proves the claim my competitors only assert.

Design my costly signal:

e List every claim my brand currently makes (expertise, taste, results,
philosophy)

e Identify which of these claims my audience secretly doesn't believe yet

e For each unbelieved claim, design one specific signal that would be expensive
or annoying for a faker to reproduce

e Pick the single signal with the highest credibility-per-effort ratio

e Build the operational plan to embed this signal into my content, profile, or
work product permanently

Inputs: My current brand claims. The claim my audience is most skeptical of. My
available time and budget for signal-building. My existing assets that could
become signals.

Output: Belief Audit > The Skepticism Map — Three Costly Signal Options > The
Highest-Leverage Signal — Permanent Embedment Plan
