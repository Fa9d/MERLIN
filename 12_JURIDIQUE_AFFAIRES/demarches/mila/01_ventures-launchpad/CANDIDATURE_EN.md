## Application

### The product — in production, verifiable today

IVIA (ivia.quebec) is a Québec platform for AI-structured listening, **deployed
and operational**: full journey from intake → booking → payment (Stripe
integrated) → audio/video session → practitioner-validated documents, with
client, practitioner and admin spaces.

The AI runs **sovereignly**: local Québec-French transcription (no voice ever
leaves the server), proprietary analysis (prosody + semantics, 11 dimensions),
and **human validation on every output** — the machine observes, hypothesizes,
orients; it never diagnoses. Privacy law (Law 25) and scope-of-practice law
(Law 21) are enforced **in code**: Québec hosting, restricted clinical
vocabulary filtered at the source, auditable access logging.

### Market and timing

Three numbers: 75% of people in distress never consult, 16,000 on the public
waiting list, 6-24 month delays. **Non-clinical** support is the missing link —
and it opens a dual market no clinical tool can serve: licensed practitioners
**and** independent companions. Add a structural trigger: Law 27 makes
workplace psychological health an employer obligation — a B2B market in
formation, which we address with a dedicated offer.

### Business model

Per-session revenue (3 roles × 3 plans pricing; per-session AI costs are
tracked — AI economics are managed, not endured), practitioner subscriptions,
and an employer offer (Law 27). Documented financial trajectory: $2.2M over
36 months, including $250-430K of non-dilutive public funding already targeted
program by program (SR&ED/CRIC, solidarity finance, innovation).

### What sets us apart

1. **Real sovereignty** — local AI by default, data in Québec: a positioning US
   platforms cannot replicate.
2. **Implemented compliance** — an enforceable ethical constitution, coded into
   the platform (anti-diagnosis, emergency escalation, anti-sycophancy).
3. **Graduated-governance engine** — full analytical capacity, exposure set per
   usage profile: one asset serving several markets.
4. **A unique consented corpus** — Québec-French support speech, with five
   research questions documented in an R&D register (including a
   shifted-temporality indicator, dated prior art).

### Founder and execution

Solo founder. The complete Phase 1 (platform, compliance, governance) was built
**nearly self-funded, at a fraction of the budget planned** in the business
plan — through methodical, documented AI-assisted development (135+ versioned
governance documents). Execution efficiency is our first demonstrated asset.

### What we seek from Launchpad

Structuring commercialization (Law 27 B2B, practitioner recruitment), access to
an entrepreneur-in-residence and first customers in the network, preparing the
raise — and anchoring IVIA in the Mila ecosystem, where our research questions
(speech, prosody, responsible AI) have natural interlocutors.
