## Pitch

IVIA is a Québec platform for AI-structured listening, **already in production**
(ivia.quebec): booking → audio session → practitioner-validated documents. All
AI runs sovereignly — local Québec-French transcription, multidimensional
analysis, human validation on every output. Privacy law (Law 25) and
scope-of-practice law (Law 21) are enforced in code, not in a PDF.

The market is opening structurally: a saturated front line, and new workplace
psychological-health obligations (Law 27) turning demand into a structured
market. We arrive with a shipped product, a consented corpus under
construction, and five research questions documented in an R&D register.

Solo founder, shipped product, early revenue: I am seeking Launchpad support to
structure commercialization and anchor IVIA in the Mila ecosystem — with the
intent of opening research collaborations as well.
