Basic Ojibwe words and phrases:
Double Vowel Chart This is how to pronounce Ojibwe words. All consanants sound the same as in English. “Zh”- sounds like the “su” in measure “a”- sounds like the “u” in sun “aa”- sounds like the “a” in father “i”- sounds like the “i” in sit “ii”- sounds like the “ee” in feet “o”- sounds like the “o” in go “oo”- sounds like the “oo” in food “e”- sounds like the “ay” in stay Questions Do you know him/her?- Gigikenimaa ina? Do you remember?- Gimikwenden ina? How are you?- Aaniin ezhi-ayaayan? How is it outside(what is the weather like)?- Aaniin ezhiwebak agwajiing? How old are you?- Aaniin endaso-biboonagiziyan? What are you called(name)?- Aaniin ezhinikaazoyan? Where are you from?- Aandi wenjibaayan? Where are you going?- Aandi ezhaayan?
